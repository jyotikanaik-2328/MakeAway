About MakeAway

MakeAway is a modern Bus Booking Management System designed to provide passengers with a fast, secure and hassle-free ticket booking experience. The platform allows users to search buses, choose seats, make secure payments and manage their journeys through an easy-to-use interface.

Our Mission

Our mission is to simplify bus travel by providing a secure, reliable and user-friendly online reservation system. MakeAway aims to make the complete booking process convenient while ensuring passenger comfort, safety and transparency.

Key Features

Bus Search

Search buses between different cities with available timings and fares.

Seat Selection

Interactive seat selection with passenger safety rules and booking restrictions.

Secure Payment

Multiple payment options with secure payment validation.

E-Ticket

Generate digital tickets instantly after successful payment.

Booking History

View previous bookings and travel information anytime.

Passenger Safety

Special seat allocation rules to improve passenger comfort and safety.
